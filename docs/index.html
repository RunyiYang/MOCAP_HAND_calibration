Research proposal · September 2026

Learn the hand’s motion.
Remember its calibration.

A proposed causal hand state-space model that combines RGB-D with an IMU glove, learns from optical motion capture, and separates fast articulation from slowly changing sensor error.

MOTION

Track the changing hand

Learn short-term joint and wrist dynamics. Keep bone lengths fixed through forward kinematics, rather than predicting unrelated 3D points.

CALIBRATION

Retain what a frame cannot

Carry mounting and pose-correction context through a recording. Update only with informative evidence and distinguish slow drift from abrupt re-donning.

UNCERTAINTY

Know when evidence is missing

Use bounded, positive-definite uncertainty and validate its coverage. Attention weights are not calibrated confidence, and a motion prior is not a new sensor.

Deployment assumptions

Primary setting. RGB-D supplies spatial observations while the glove supplies motion evidence. MoCap is absent at inference. The estimator corrects pose and persistent calibration state, with depth enabled only after native metric values are verified.

02 / Learning objectives

Supervise the state.
Respect the measurement.

Losses are masked, scale-normalized, and introduced in stages. Unsupported labels never become invented targets.

Geometric pose

Robust MoCap joint/fingertip loss through FK. Independent root loss and SO(3) rotation loss only where valid references exist.

Marker observations

Predict surface markers through calibrated attachment offsets. A glove reflector is not an anatomical joint center.

Visual consistency

2D reprojection and visibility-aware metric-depth consistency, with the real camera model and invalid-pixel masks.

Sensor consistency

Model vendor-pose corruption initially. Add gyro/specific-force observation losses only when raw IMU measurements are available.

Dynamics + calibration

Match trustworthy motion derivatives, regularize slow calibration changes, and preserve anatomical constraints without suppressing real motion.

Prediction + uncertainty

Train future-state rollouts with all future inputs removed. Use likelihood and held-out coverage to evaluate uncertainty.

Important distinction: reconstructing a plausible pose under occlusion is not proof of drift-free tracking. Camera-outage filtering and all-input-free prediction require separate evaluations.

03 / Training program

Earn complexity in stages.

Run a small supervised pilot before larger experiments. The proposal does not launch training jobs or presume a large paired dataset already exists.

Stage A

Audit + baselines

Verify clocks, geometry, marker semantics and splits. Establish vendor, fixed-calibration, visual, filter and causal-GRU baselines.

Gate: clean-segment fit, causality tests and no MoCap leakage.

Stage B

Supervised fusion

Freeze visual features. Train compact glove/motion/observation heads on reliable MoCap supervision.

Gate: held-out session improvement without hidden latency or coverage costs.

Stage C

Persistent dynamics

Add slow calibration, realistic drift, contiguous outages and future rollouts. Carry state across training chunks.

Gate: measurable prediction and reacquisition gains over matched baselines.

Stage D

Raw sensors + runtime

Collect raw IMUs; add physical observation losses, delayed-vision correction and safe online adaptation.

Gate: independent long-session tests and measured end-to-end latency.

04 / Dataset readiness

Begin with the data we actually have.

The existing repository provides valuable reviewed calibration evidence. Its visualization protocols must not be mistaken for independent learned-model evaluation.

Solved poses are not raw IMUs

The old captures contain vendor-solved keypoints. The first version corrects those poses; physical inertial-bias modeling needs new raw streams.

Surface markers are not joint GT

Take_007 supplies surface landmarks and a constructed wrist. Full anatomical rotations cannot simply be read from these points.

Preview depth is not metric depth

Use validated original depth, camera intrinsics and timing. A lossy preview must not drive metric depth losses.

Conditioned videos are diagnostics

MoCap wrist conditioning and display-only offsets remain explicitly labeled. They are excluded from independent deployment inference.
